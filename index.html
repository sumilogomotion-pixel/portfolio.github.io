The process

Handmade where it matters. Precise where it counts.

The brush is not decoration. It is the source material for a controlled brand system.

Not a brush font.
Not a generic template.

01

Brush

Original marks are drawn by hand with physical ink on paper to create forms with real pressure, rhythm and irregularity.

02

Refine

Shape, balance and legibility are edited digitally so the work can function as a real identity — not just an expressive mark.

03

Motion

The reveal is designed around the character of the logo. Timing and texture support the identity instead of overpowering it.

About
